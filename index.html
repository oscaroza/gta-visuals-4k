GTA Visuals 4K

Personal desktop tool for short-form video creation.

GTA Visuals 4K runs locally on its owner's computer. It turns long videos into short vertical clips with subtitles, framing and music, then lets the owner upload them to their own TikTok account, either as a draft to finish in the TikTok app or as a direct post.

Videos are only sent to TikTok when the user explicitly clicks “Send to drafts” or “Post”. Before posting, the user chooses who can see the video and the interaction settings.
