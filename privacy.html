Privacy Policy

GTA Visuals 4K · Last updated: November 19, 2026

What the app is

GTA Visuals 4K runs locally on the owner's computer. It has no server, and it does not sell or share any data.

Data received from TikTok

When you connect your TikTok account, the app receives an access token and your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar). They are stored only on your computer and used only to upload the videos you choose and to show which account is connected.

Your videos

Your videos and clips stay on your computer. A video is sent to TikTok only when you click “Send to drafts” or “Post”.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data at any time by disconnecting your account in the app settings, or by revoking access in your TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).
